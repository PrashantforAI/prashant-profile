# Recruiter Strategy — How to Use This Page

## The Problem This Page Solves
Recruiters spend 6–10 seconds scanning a LinkedIn profile. A portfolio page gives them a reason to stay longer, see real work, and remember you. It also signals that you're someone who builds — not just talks.

---

## Where to Share This Page

### 1. LinkedIn Profile
- Add the URL to your **About section** ("See my work: [url]")
- Add it as a **Featured link** at the top of your profile
- Add it to your **Contact Info**

### 2. Email Signature
Add a one-liner: `Portfolio: [url]`

### 3. Job Applications
- When applying via email, add the page link to your email body
- When applying via portals, paste the URL in the "website" or "portfolio" field

### 4. Cold Outreach to Recruiters
Use this message template when reaching out on LinkedIn:
> "Hi [Name], I'm an AI Product Builder and Operations Leader based in Mumbai, actively exploring full-time opportunities. I've built a few live AI products — you can see them at [url]. Would love to connect if you're working with companies that could use someone with this background."

### 5. WhatsApp / Text Outreach
The Open Graph tags ensure the page shows a rich preview when shared on WhatsApp — your name, description, and photo will appear automatically.

---

## What Recruiters Will See (in order)

1. **Hero** — Your name, role, and one strong sentence. They know who you are in 3 seconds.
2. **About** — Stats that prove impact. 40% reduction, 18 titles, 29 sessions, 3 live products.
3. **Projects** — Live links they can actually click. This is rare. Most candidates don't have this.
4. **Experience** — Familiar format. They can scan it like a resume.
5. **Skills** — Confirms tech stack at a glance.
6. **Contact** — Easy way to reach you. No friction.

---

## What Makes This Page Stand Out

- **Live products with real URLs** — Most AI people talk. You ship.
- **Specific numbers** — 40%, 18, 29, 3+ — these are memorable
- **Clear positioning** — You're not just "AI enthusiast" — you're a builder + operator
- **One-page simplicity** — No PDFs, no navigation confusion
- **Dark minimal design** — Stands out from generic templates
