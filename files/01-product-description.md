# Product Description — Prashant Mishra Personal Profile Page

## What This Page Is
A personal portfolio and profile page for **Prashant Mishra** — designed to attract recruiters, hiring managers, and potential clients. It serves as a single, authoritative link that communicates who Prashant is, what he builds, and why someone should hire or work with him.

## Primary Goal
Convert a recruiter or hiring manager visiting the page into someone who reaches out — either for a full-time role or a freelance/consulting engagement.

## Target Audience
- **Recruiters** at tech companies, AI startups, or ops-heavy businesses
- **Hiring managers** looking for an AI-savvy operations or product leader
- **Potential clients** exploring AI consulting or automation services
- **Collaborators** in the AI/startup ecosystem

## Positioning
Prashant is positioned as **four things simultaneously**:
1. **AI Product Builder** — He doesn't just use AI tools, he builds AI-native products (AIBNB, FrameFlow)
2. **Freelance AI Consultant** — Through AiTribe, he helps D2C brands and businesses with AI media and automation
3. **Operations Leader** — 3+ years leading end-to-end ops at Pine Stays, with measurable business impact
4. **Project Manager** — Coordinated cross-functional delivery across publishing, EdTech, and tech platforms

## Tone & Messaging
- **Confident but not arrogant** — Let the work speak
- **Practical and results-driven** — Numbers, outcomes, live links
- **Forward-looking** — AI-native, vibe coder, builder mindset
- **Human** — Not a resume dump; a real person who builds things

## Key Message (Hero Statement)
> "I don't just use AI — I build with it."

## What Makes Prashant Different
- Rare combo: business operations depth + hands-on AI product building
- Has live, working products (not just side projects in a README)
- Understands both the technical and business side of AI
- Has shipped: 18 published titles, 29 live training sessions, AI tools reducing workload by 40%

## Success Metrics for This Page
- Recruiter spends more than 90 seconds on the page
- Clicks through to at least one project
- Reaches out via email or LinkedIn
